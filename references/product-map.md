# TokenPass Product Map

Live public-site capabilities and messaging checked on 2026-11-25. Counts, prices, benefits, and policies change; confirm them on the linked page before publishing new copy.

## Positioning

TokenPass helps people keep using the AI model they want while comparing the cost and terms of different public routes. It brings together same-model pricing, free-tier conditions, provider/channel status, and user-selected email monitoring. The product promise is: **make the price difference visible, make the risk legible, and save users from checking every pricing page by hand.**

The public homepage displayed 307 models when reviewed; public-facing copy should say “300+” because the catalog changes. The site is an independent information service. It does not provide model inference, take payment for provider usage, or guarantee third-party service quality.

## Strongest differentiators to lead with

1. **Same model, different routes.** First compare providers for the model the user already relies on; changing model quality is not required just to investigate savings.
2. **Usable free credits, not vanity totals.** Show rate limits, peak behavior, card requirements, expiration, and data-use conditions alongside credits.
3. **Price plus risk, in the same decision.** Curated board and deep-discount information have distinct labels; varying provider credentials are not hidden behind a low price.
4. **A continuous same-model comparison model.** Track the user's current provider and model, alert when a more affordable route for the same model appears or the current route raises its price, then bring the user back to TokenPass to compare and choose. In theory, this lets users keep choosing the lowest-priced tracked option.
5. **Ranking policy is public.** TokenPass discloses its referral revenue and says commissions do not affect rankings; the About page also says platforms without referral deals may still be listed.
6. **A quick VS path for model choice.** Homepage visitors can compare just two selected models without first working through the full provider table.
7. **Participation improves the catalog.** Users can submit missing providers or routes for verification and earn membership rewards under current rules.
8. **Low-friction signup.** Public comparison needs no account. For email alerts, an email address is the only personal information requested; model and notification preferences are selected on the page. No API key is required.

## Language and audience guidance

- Chinese copy may say “国内平台” when referring to platforms based in China.
- The homepage language selector showed seven options when checked on 2026-11-25: English, Español, Français, Deutsch, 中文, 日本語, 한국어. English copy can say “seven language options: English, Spanish, French, German, Chinese, Japanese, and Korean,” and describe provider coverage as “providers in China and around the world.” Avoid translating “国内” as an undefined “domestic” audience or implying the English product is only for China.

These differentiators come from current public-site surfaces. Avoid generic claims such as “all prices are real-time,” “lowest price guaranteed,” “safe,” or “all providers covered.”

## Claims and caveats

- Counts and prices are dynamic. Use “300+ models” and direct readers to the live catalog; do not freeze a scraped count into long-lived copy.
- The price comparison page and savings board describe different multiplier baselines. Name the relevant page and its method.
- The free-credit page states daily refresh, but each provider’s own terms are authoritative. Do not promise that every policy change will be caught immediately.
- Treat “curated” and “verified” as descriptions of TokenPass's own review/listing process. They are not an independent certification of safety or performance.
- Deep-discount channels are explicitly labeled as varying in credentials and requiring user verification. Do not call them authorized, risk-free, or recommended without evidence for the specific channel.
- Monitoring/removal language describes a process, not an uptime, detection, refund, or loss-prevention guarantee.
- Read current public disclosures before describing referral, affiliate, membership, refund, privacy, or reward terms.
